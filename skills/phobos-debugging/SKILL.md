# phobos-debugging

Record the observed failure, expected result and exact environment. Trace inputs through the relevant component boundaries. Form one falsifiable hypothesis and inspect targeted evidence before editing. Implement the smallest fix that explains the failure; avoid speculative rewrites. Then reproduce the original failure and verify the corrected outcome. Record a procedure draft only after success; keep uncertainty and source references in memory.
